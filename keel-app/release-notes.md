Budgets that fit how you pay.

- Spread a payment over several months. Pay 6 months of car insurance at once and each of those
  6 months counts one equal share, so no month shows red and no month shows empty. Set it on any
  expense with "Spread over", or turn on "Spread big payments" for a category and Keel works out
  the months from your budget. Cash flow still shows the payment on the day it left your account.
- Enter any category's budget per month or per year with the /mo and /yr switch.
- Organize your budget: move categories up and down, move them between groups, and add, rename,
  reorder or delete groups. The Budget page follows the same order.

Downloads: Mac, Keel-2.4.0-mac.dmg (Apple Silicon and Intel). Windows, Keel-Setup-2.4.0.exe.
If you have 2.3.0, Keel offers this update on its own.
