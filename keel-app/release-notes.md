Keel now keeps itself up to date.

- Windows: the installed app downloads new versions in the background and asks you to restart.
- Mac: the app tells you when a new version is out and opens the download.
- Categories page: see your app version and check for updates any time.

Install this version by hand once. Updates after this one come to you.

Downloads: Mac, Keel-2.3.0-mac.dmg (Apple Silicon and Intel). Windows, Keel-Setup-2.3.0.exe.
